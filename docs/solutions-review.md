# Solutions & Services Iteration Tracker

*This document serves as a working scratchpad for the Quarterly Business Review (QBR). We will use this to track, iterate, and refine the UX of the "Services" offered by FYI Lab.*

## Current State (V1)
Currently, the services are grouped into four broad categories:
1. **Custom Data Visualization** (Chaos to Clarity)
2. **Automated Dashboard Design** (Input to Impact)
3. **Data Storytelling & Training** (Data to Wow)
4. **Strategic Workshopping** (Ideas to Core Message)

**Diagnostic UX Review:**
- The combination of "Data Storytelling" (Done For You) and "Training" (Done With You / Teaching) targets two highly distinct buyer mindsets.
- An executive who wants a finished data story is not the same buyer as a manager looking to upskill a data team.

## Proposed Future States (Iteration Workspace)

### Option A: The "Done For You" vs. "Done With You" Split
*Separating purely by engagement model.*

**Done For You (Execution)**
- **Bespoke Data Stories & Explainers:** Transforming dense research into accessible, interactive narratives the public and press can instantly grasp.
- **Automated Dashboards:** Creating operational views that reduce cognitive load and empower faster executive decisions.

**Done With You (Advisory & Capability)**
- **Strategic Visualization Workshops:** Untangling your complex ideas into a clear core message before you build.
- **Team Training:** Teaching your internal data teams the fundamentals of visual clarity and data journalism.

### Option B: The "Output" Split
*Separating by the final deliverable.*

1. **Interactive Scrollytelling & Explainers**
2. **Operational Dashboards**
3. **Strategic Whiteboarding / Roadmapping**
4. **Data Literacy Training**
